# Njin Client & Internal Onboarding Flow

## Prospecting

### 1. Initial Sales Call & Interest

- **Client Experience:** Books call via sales CRM
- **Internal Process:** Added to GHL pipeline
- **AI Automations:** Research, Lead Scoring, Meeting Transcripts
- **Key Note:** Anchor call around an Activation Promise. Example: "Within 7 days of payment, you'll have X running and already saving you Y hours." Certainty > Information.
- **Location:** GHL
- **Phase:** 2
- **Responsible:** Sales

### 2. Brief OR Reverse Brief

- **Client Experience:** Discovery conversation
- **Internal Process:** Reverse Brief drafted and sent to client for approval
- **Timeline:** No more than 24 business hours after last contact
- **AI Automations:** Reverse Brief - drafted as an email
- **Key Note:** Always structure the reverse brief as: Problem > Cost of Problem
- **Location:** GHL
- **Phase:** 2
- **Responsible:** Sales

## Project Initiation

### 3. Scope of Work

- **Client Experience:** Receives reverse brief which acts as confirmation of high-level requirements
- **Internal Process:** Define deliverables and resources
- **AI Automations:** Draft Scope of Work as a document (Google Doc)
- **Trigger:** Manual - client approves reverse brief and staff moved to next stage of the pipeline
- **Key Note:** Always include "Earliest Possible Win" as a deliverable
- **Location:** GHL
- **Phase:** 2
- **Responsible:** Sales/Product

### 4. Proposal

- **Client Experience:** Receives pricing, timeline, deliverables
- **Internal Process:** Proposal drafted
- **AI Automations:** Proposal PDF
- **Key Note:** Translate deliverables into outcomes (e.g. "This automation saves 40 hours/month" instead of "Setup chatbot")
- **Location:** GHL
- **Phase:** 2
- **Responsible:** Sales

### 5. Verbal Go-Ahead

- **Client Experience:** Gives verbal approval on call
- **Internal Process:** Marked in CRM to trigger contract automation
- **AI Automations:** CRM Approval Log
- **Phase:** N/A
- **Responsible:** Sales

### 6. Automated Contract Send

- **Process:** Generated from proposal data, sent for e-signature
- **AI Automations:** Draft Contract, Signed Contract
- **Phase:** 3
- **Responsible:** Operations

### 7. Automated Invoice Send

- **Process:** Sent immediately after contract signed via Xero integration
- **AI Automations:** Invoice
- **Phase:** 3
- **Responsible:** Operations

### 8. Payment Nurture & Confirmation

- **Process:** Custom reminder sequence + Xero reminders, auto-reconciliation when paid
- **AI Automations:** Payment Confirmation Log
- **Phase:** 3
- **Responsible:** Operations

## Project Planning

### 9. Free Gift Dispatch

- **Process:** Triggered only on payment confirmation
- **AI Automations:** Gift Order Confirmation
- **Client Message:** "Whilst you enjoy this, we're already building your first automation"
- **Important:** Large corporates or government agencies cannot accept gifts
- **Phase:** 3

### 10. Dashboard Account Creation

- **Process:** Internal "Vibe-Coded" dashboard; credentials sent in group chat (or via email for phase 1)
- **AI Automations:** Dashboard Access Details
- **Phase:** 1

### 11. Dashboard Onboarding

- **Process:** Collects key info interactively; replaces old questionnaires
- **AI Automations:** Intake Data Records
- **Phase:** 1

### 12. Group Chat Setup (WhatsApp or Slack)

- **Process:** Created post-payment; includes welcome message
- **AI Automations:** Welcome Message Template
- **Important:** This might need to be other methods (email or SMS) for corporate phones - needs to be checked
- **Phase:** 3

### 13. Vector Database Logging

- **Process:** All meeting transcripts + docs stored and tagged by client
- **AI Automations:** Indexed Transcript & Document Library
- **Phase:** 2

### 14. Onboarding Activation Call (within 72 hours)

- **Purpose:** Streamlined, professional, confidence-inspiring. This is not about showing deliverables. It's about removing friction and setting expectations.
- **Objectives:**
  - Collect final access, inputs, workflows
  - Show organisation and clarity
  - Anchor the timeline for first deliverables
  - Build trust that delivery is already in motion
- **Structure:**
  1. **Welcome & Framing:** "This call gets everything aligned so we can deliver your first XYZ at kickoff"
  2. **Confirm Goals:** Re-state the top outcomes they want
  3. **Information Gathering:** Collect all required access, logins, datasets
  4. **Dashboard Overview:** Show where they'll track progress, log tickets
  5. **Timeline Anchor:** Reiterate kickoff date + promise of first deliverables
  6. **Confidence Close:** "We now have everything we need. Expect your first XYZ live at kickoff"
- **Client Experience:** Roadmap walkthrough, resource access
- **Internal Process:** PM/BA gathers and finalises all requirements
- **AI Automations:** Context Pack, Roadmap Sheet, Access Guide
- **Phase:** 2
- **Responsible:** Sales (supported by Customer Success)

### 15. Internal Planning

- **Process:** Internal team alignment and resource allocation
- **AI Automations:** Internal Planning Documents
- **Phase:** 2
- **Responsible:** Customer Success (supported by Sales)

### 16. Kickoff Scheduling Automation

- **Process:** Triggered after onboarding; auto-create kickoff event
- **AI Automations:** Kickoff Agenda
- **Phase:** 2

## Project Execution

### 17. Kickoff Call (1 week after onboarding)

- **Objectives:**
  - Deliver quick win
  - Reconfirm alignment
  - Show the plan for all remaining deliverables
  - Build certainty for long-term success
- **Structure:**
  1. **Strategic Alignment:** Confirm outcomes and priorities
  2. **Roadmap Reveal:** Show full timeline and milestones
  3. **Value Framing:** Translate deliverables into hours saved, costs cut, errors reduced
  4. **Close with Certainty:** "You've already got X. Next, you'll get Y by [date]"
- **Client Experience:** Confirms strategy & project plan
- **AI Automations:** Sprint Plan, Technical Spec, Resource Sheet
- **Phase:** 2
- **Responsible:** Customer Success (supported by PMO)

### 18. Sprint Scheduling Automation

- **Process:** Post-kickoff, auto-create recurring sprint events & dashboard sections
- **AI Automations:** Sprint Calendar
- **Phase:** 4
- **Responsible:** PMO

### 19. Optional Training Module (if adoption required)

- **Client Experience:** Training delivered
- **Internal Process:** Provide live/recorded workshop
- **Framing:** Position as bonus value: "We don't just build, we empower your team to scale with it"
- **Responsible:** Customer Success

### 20. First Delivery Cycle (Fortnightly Sprints)

- **Client Experience:** Reviews deliverables
- **Internal Process:** Executes sprints
- **AI Automations:** Roadmap Update, Sprint Report, QA Results, Change Log
- **Phase:** 4
- **Responsible:** PMO

### 21. Ongoing Engagement - Customer Success Management

- **Client Experience:** Attends reviews
- **Internal Process:** Tracks KPIs
- **AI Automations:** Feedback Summary, KPI Tracker, Updated Notes
- **Key Note:** Frame reviews as "Results & ROI Sessions." Always:
  - Re-sell why they bought
  - Show ROI
  - Seed next-stage upsell
- **Phase:** 4
- **Responsible:** Customer Success

## Closure

### 22. Project Phase Completion

- **Client Experience:** Receives final deliverables & success report
- **Internal Process:** Retrospective & upsell planning
- **AI Automations:** Final QA Checklist, Success Report, Customer Sign-off, Next Stage Proposal
- **Phase:** 4
- **Responsible:** Customer Success (supported by PMO)

### 23. Next Stage Proposal

- **Client Experience:** Receives continuity/upsell/cross-sell offer immediately with Success Report
- **Internal Process:** Draft upsell
- **AI Automations:** Proposal template
- **Key Note:** Use Ascension > Selling > Reselling loop:
  - **Ascension:** Offer more complex solutions
  - **Selling:** Highlight ROI so far
  - **Reselling:** Push continuity (retain them on managed service)
- **Responsible:** Sales (supported by Customer Success)
