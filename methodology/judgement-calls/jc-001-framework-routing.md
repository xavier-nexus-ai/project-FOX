# Judgement Call 1: Which Framework to Use for an Engagement

**Title:** Framework Routing — Matching Engagement Type to the Right Framework

**The Decision:** Given what the client needs, which of Njin's deployment frameworks should govern this engagement?

**Signals:**

| Signal | Framework |
|--------|-----------|
| Client wants more leads, better conversion, higher retention — outcomes tied to revenue pillars | **Njin Method (TORQUE)** |
| Client has structured IP, methodology, or repeatable workflows they want to systematise into AI agents | **AI Orchestration** |
| Client needs a web app, internal tool, dashboard, or product built | **VIBE OS** |
| Client needs a new website, landing page, marketing funnel, or online presence | **AetherFlow** |
| Client is a SaaS business needing growth strategy, acquisition loops, or retention systems | **JK Growth** |
| Client needs a personal or team AI assistant — a chief of staff, research agent, or operating companion | **OpenClaw** |

**Edge Cases:**
- A consultancy that wants *both* a playbook (revenue) *and* their methodology systematised into agents — start with Njin Method TORQUE to fix revenue first. Once playbooks are live and generating results, layer AI Orchestration on top. Don't run them in parallel on day one.
- A client who needs a website *and* CRM automation — AetherFlow for the website, Njin Method for the sales pipeline. Treat them as separate workstreams.
- A client asking for "AI" vaguely — diagnose their actual constraint using the Core 12 before assigning a framework. AI is the tool, not the goal.
- An internal Njin build (like the PM framework) — AI Orchestration, because you're extracting and systematising IP.

**Default:** When in doubt, default to **Njin Method (TORQUE)**. Most paying clients come to Njin because something in their revenue funnel is broken. That's a playbook problem first.

**Exceptions:**
- If the client has zero existing IP or methodology, AI Orchestration won't work — there's nothing to extract. Run TORQUE first.
- If the engagement is purely technical (build a tool, no methodology), skip Njin Method entirely and go VIBE OS.
