# Framework Reference: JK Growth

## Purpose

JK Growth is a SaaS growth framework that helps founders build predictable, scalable revenue through three growth engines: Product Led Growth (PLG), AI Led Growth (ALG), and Community Led Growth (CLG). It runs ICP-first, diagnoses the primary revenue constraint, selects the right growth engine, then executes from discovery through to scaling.

## Target Audience

SaaS founders and product businesses at any stage — from ideation through to scaling. Fits when:

- The client has a SaaS product (or is building one)
- They need a structured path from idea or early revenue to predictable growth
- They want to know where their revenue is leaking and how to fix it
- They're open to PLG, ALG, or CLG as a growth mechanism

Client signals that fit JK Growth:
- "We have a SaaS and growth has stalled"
- "We just launched and don't know how to get customers"
- "We have users but they're not converting to paid"
- "Our churn is too high"
- "We want to build a community around our product"
- "We want AI to help us automate our growth processes"
- "We're pre-launch and need a go-to-market plan"

## Phases / Methodology

JK Growth runs five sequential phases. The client's current stage determines where they enter, but the framework progresses forward from that entry point.

**Discovery**
Define who the product is for, validate the idea or market signal, and identify the primary growth engine. Covers ICP building, baseline metric collection (12 Core Numbers), constraint diagnosis, and growth engine selection. Outputs the cos.yaml with confirmed baseline data.

**Product Fundamentals**
Build the lean canvas, define core metrics and targets, and establish product-market fit signals. Outputs lean canvas, metric baselines, and a feature prioritisation framework.

**Launch Strategy**
Build the go-to-market plan, select channels, and pick a launch play (pre-sale, beta waitlist, Product Hunt, or direct launch). Outputs the GTM plan, channel strategy, and launch playbook.

**Execution**
Execute the launch, iterate on metrics in real time, and build growth loops. Outputs live campaigns, metric dashboards, and active growth loops.

**Scaling**
Optimise whichever growth engine is working, expand channels, and build the team structure to sustain growth without founder dependency.

### Growth Engine Selection

The framework routes strategy based on which engine fits the product and market:

| Engine | What It Is | When to Use |
|--------|-----------|-------------|
| **PLG** | Product drives acquisition, activation, and expansion. Freemium, trials, viral loops. | Product has natural shareability or self-serve value |
| **ALG** | AI automates and optimises growth workflows — outreach, personalisation, lead nurture, conversational AI. | Team is small, manual growth processes exist, AI can replace or accelerate human effort |
| **CLG** | Community drives acquisition and retention. Engagement rituals, community-to-revenue loops. | ICP has strong identity and gathering behaviour, product benefits from peer learning |

Most engagements use a hybrid — the framework identifies the primary engine and supporting engines.

## Key Deliverables

- ICP profile (client-confirmed, not inferred)
- 12 Core Numbers baseline (confirmed, unknown, or client-estimate — never analyst-inferred)
- Constraint diagnosis (primary and secondary revenue leak)
- Lean canvas
- Go-to-market plan
- Channel strategy
- Launch playbook with selected launch play
- Live campaigns and growth loops
- Metric dashboards
- Scaling playbook
- Team structure recommendation
- cos.yaml — full engagement state tracked throughout

## Blocking Gates

- **Hard Gates (Pre-Engagement)** — The Qualification Analyst checks four gates before investing time: client can prove demand exists, has some sales process, is the decision maker, and has budget capacity. If any gate fails, disqualify. Do not force fit.

- **12 Core Numbers Gate** — COS cannot be created until all 12 Core Numbers are collected. Numbers must be client-confirmed. "Unknown" is acceptable. Analyst-inferred values are never acceptable. This gate is non-negotiable.

- **Voice Gate** — The client's tone of voice must be captured and codified before significant content output begins. Flagged as `PENDING` or `PASSED` in COS.

- **Data Access Gate** — Access to the client's CRM, analytics, or ad platform data is required before metric validation can complete. Flagged in COS.

- **Buy-In Check** — Before proposing, the Qualification Analyst asks: "On a scale of 1-10, how committed are you to fixing this in the next 90 days?" Threshold is 7. Below 7 — explore what's blocking commitment before proceeding.

- **Phase Progression Gates** — Each phase has defined deliverables tracked in COS. The orchestrator checks phase deliverables before advancing. No phase can be marked complete with outstanding deliverables.

## Typical Engagement Shape

- **Duration:** Growth Strategy Sprint — 4 to 6 weeks. 90-Day Growth Program — 3 months. Ongoing Advisory — monthly retainer.
- **Client time commitment:** Medium to high. Weekly calls or check-ins during active phases. Client must provide data for the 12 Core Numbers — no shortcuts.
- **Pricing model:** Three standard offer packages:
  - Growth Strategy Sprint — Conversion constraint with good lead flow
  - 90-Day Growth Program — Acquisition and conversion constraints combined
  - Ongoing Advisory — Full transformation across all four revenue pillars over time
- **Team involved:** Qualification Analyst leads Discovery. Orchestrator routes to growth engine specialists (PLG, ALG, CLG) for execution. Sales and marketing specialists activate for launch.

## How to Scope This Framework

A PM needs the following before scoping a JK Growth engagement:

**Must-have inputs:**
- What stage is the client at? (Ideation / Pre-launch / Post-launch / Scaling)
- What growth engine fits — PLG, ALG, CLG, or hybrid?
- What is the primary revenue constraint? (Acquisition / Conversion / Monetisation / Retention)
- What does the client know about their own metrics? (12 Core Numbers availability)
- What is the client's 90-day goal?
- What offer package fits the diagnosis?

**Scope questions to ask:**
- What's the product and who is it for?
- What does revenue look like today? (MRR, users, churn, CAC)
- Where do new customers currently come from?
- What's the biggest leak — not enough leads, leads not converting, small deal sizes, or customers leaving?
- Is the constraint demand (not enough people in the pipeline) or supply (genuinely at capacity)?
- What does the client think is broken, and what do the numbers say?

**Critical scoping discipline — Demand vs Supply validation:**
70–80% of SaaS businesses are demand-constrained, not supply-constrained. When a client says they need "better automation" or "efficiency improvements", probe before accepting that framing. Ask: "If 10 qualified leads landed in your inbox tomorrow, could you service them?" If yes, the problem is demand, not operations.

**Common missing-info pitfalls:**
- Accepting the client's self-diagnosis without validating against the 12 Core Numbers
- Not checking buy-in level before scoping — a 5/10 committed client will stall the engagement
- Missing data: if the client doesn't know their churn rate, CAC, or close rate, flag it as "Unknown" in scope and budget for an Activation phase to gather it
- Assuming PLG without checking if the product actually has self-serve value
- Scoping Scaling work before the client has completed Discovery and Product Fundamentals

## Signals This is the Right Framework

- The client has a SaaS product (or is building one)
- They need structured growth strategy, not just a marketing campaign
- Revenue is stalled, inconsistent, or not yet started
- The 12 Core Numbers reveal a diagnosable constraint
- They want PLG, ALG, or CLG as a growth mechanism
- They're willing to share data and have a 7+ buy-in level
- The engagement needs a phase-based operating system (COS) to track progress

## Signals This is NOT the Right Framework

- The deliverable is a website or funnel build — use AetherFlow instead
- The client is building a non-SaaS software product — use VIBE OS instead
- The client wants traditional marketing retainer work without growth strategy — wrong fit
- Buy-in score is below 7 and they can't articulate why they want to grow — not ready
- There is no product yet and no plan to build one — too early even for Discovery
