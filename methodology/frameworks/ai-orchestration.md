# Framework Reference: AI Orchestration (90-Day AI System Build)

## Purpose

The AI Orchestration framework guides businesses through extracting their intellectual property and building it into a production-ready, deployable AI framework. The output is not a chatbot or a prompt library — it is a complete system of specialist AI agents that think, decide, and deliver using the client's own methodology, language, and judgement calls.

---

## Target Audience

**Primary:** Businesses that have structured IP, repeatable delivery workflows, and expert knowledge currently locked in people's heads. They want to scale delivery without proportionally scaling headcount. Typically $1M+ revenue, established methodologies or processes, and a desire to productise their expertise.

**Business types this fits:**
- Agencies and consultancies with documented delivery processes
- Coaching and education businesses with proprietary frameworks or course curricula
- Professional services firms (legal, accounting, engineering) with compliance-aware workflows
- SaaS companies with defined onboarding and customer success processes
- Training organisations with modular, repeatable delivery

**Client signals that indicate this framework fits:**
- "We deliver great results but can't scale because it all runs through senior people"
- "We have a framework/methodology but it's not documented in a usable way"
- "I want to package what I know so I can deliver it to more clients"
- "We need junior staff to deliver at senior quality"
- "We want to build an AI-powered product from our existing IP"
- Client has named frameworks, SOPs, or proprietary terminology they use with clients

## Phases / Methodology

The engagement runs 90 days across three phases. Phases are sequential — blocking gates sit between each one.

### Phase 1: Immersion (Weeks 1–4)

Get the client AI-literate, configure their technical environment, and deliver a tangible quick win. This phase ensures the client can participate meaningfully in Mapping. Without it, IP extraction interviews are shallow.

- **Weeks 1–2:** AI literacy education (adapted to the client's current level). Covers agents, skills, COS, multi-chat workflows, and Claude Code as a platform.
- **Weeks 2–3:** Industry AI analysis — landscape, competitor usage, opportunity gaps, risks, 12-month trend forecast.
- **Weeks 3–4:** Stack configuration — Claude Code, MCP servers, environment validation, hands-on training.
- **Week 4:** Quick win build — client builds something tangible (proposal generator, quiz, calculator) to build confidence before Mapping.

**Key deliverables:** AI Possibilities Map, Industry AI Report, Stack Validation Report, Quick Win prototype.

### Phase 2: Mapping (Weeks 5–8)

Extract all IP and design the system architecture. This is the most important phase — shallow extraction produces a generic system. Deep extraction produces something that genuinely thinks like the client's best people.

- **Weeks 5–6:** IP extraction — multiple 60–90 minute interview sessions to extract frameworks, SOPs, judgement calls, vocabulary, and delivery sequences.
- **Weeks 6–7:** Methodology architecture — structure extracted IP into a named, phased methodology (3–7 phases). Client names their methodology — this is a branding decision they own.
- **Week 7:** Diagnostics design — create intake assessments, scoring rubrics, phase progress diagnostics, and triage tools.
- **Weeks 7–8:** Constraint mapping — classify every process as fully automated, AI-assisted, AI-augmented, or human-only.
- **Weeks 7–8:** Leverage planning — design the commercial model: pricing, offer packaging, delivery redesign, financial projections, scaling roadmap.

**Key deliverables:** IP Vault (all extracted IP), Named Methodology with phase definitions, Diagnostic Tools, Constraint Map, AI-Human Boundary document, Leverage Plan, Scaling Roadmap.

### Phase 3: Transformation (Weeks 9–12)

Build the complete framework and deploy it. Client involvement drops here — the heavy lifting is on the delivery side. The client's role shifts to reviewing, approving, and learning to operate the framework.

- **Weeks 9–11:** Framework construction — build all 10 components in a prescribed sequence (COS schema → architecture → agents → skills → methodology wiring → diagnostics → playbooks → gates → deploy script → CLAUDE.md).
- **Weeks 11–12:** QA validation — independent validation across 10 categories. Binary pass/fail. If anything fails, it routes back for targeted fixes before re-validation.
- **Week 12:** Deployment and training — deploy the framework to a real customer project, train the client to operate and maintain it independently.

**Key deliverables:** Complete AI framework (all 10 components), QA Report, Deploy Script, trained client, first customer deployment.

## Key Deliverables

Every engagement produces 10 core deliverables the client owns permanently:

- **Named Methodology** — Branded, phased transformation with clear progression criteria
- **Diagnostic Instruments** — Intake assessment, scoring rubrics, phase progress checkpoints
- **Playbooks** — Step-by-step operational guides per methodology phase
- **Templates** — Document and deliverable templates the framework produces
- **AI Agents** — Specialist AI personas with client IP, voice, and decision heuristics baked in
- **Skills** — Modular expertise modules (SOPs and judgement calls as reusable knowledge)
- **Workflows** — Phase execution sequences defining agent coordination
- **Tasks** — Discrete work units within each workflow
- **Client Operating System (COS)** — YAML state file tracking every customer's progress
- **Deploy Script + Scaling Roadmap** — One-command deployment to new customer projects, plus commercial scaling plan

## Blocking Gates

| Gate | Required Conditions |
|------|---------------------|
| **Immersion Exit** | AI literacy confirmed, AI Possibilities Map done, Industry Report done, stack validated, client can independently load an agent and navigate the output, quick win built |
| **IP Extraction Complete** | Business workflows mapped end-to-end, 2+ frameworks extracted, 3+ SOPs extracted, 5+ judgement calls extracted, methodology named |
| **Mapping Exit** | All IP extraction gates passed, methodology structured with phases, diagnostics designed, constraint map created, leverage plan completed with financial projections |
| **Components Built** | All agents, skills, COS schema, templates, workflows, tasks, checklists, CLAUDE.md, and deploy script created |
| **QA Passed** | Framework assembled, QA validation passed (all critical checks green), deploy script tested, client trained, first deployment successful |

**Gate enforcement is strict.** Building on incomplete foundations is the most common failure mode. If Immersion runs long, flag it early — compressing Mapping is not an option.

## How to Scope This Framework

Before scoping, a PM needs to know:

1. **What IP does the client have?** Frameworks, SOPs, proprietary methodologies, named processes. The more structured the existing IP, the faster Mapping goes. If IP is entirely in people's heads and undocumented, budget more extraction sessions.
2. **What business type are they?** Education/course businesses have more templated delivery; agencies and consultancies require more bespoke extraction. See SOP Section 9 for business-type variations.
3. **How AI-literate is the client?** Beginner clients need more time in Immersion. Assess before kick-off. If they're already using Claude Projects and understand agents, Weeks 1–2 compress.
4. **How many agents will the framework need?** Typical is 5–10 agents depending on methodology complexity. More phases = more agents. Note this in the scope — it affects Transformation build time.
5. **What platform are they building on?** Claude Code is the primary platform. If the client needs Gemini or ChatGPT compatibility, account for additional configuration.
6. **Do they have source materials?** Existing courses, SOPs, frameworks, transcripts, and documents in `docs/background/` accelerate Cipher's extraction work significantly. If they have nothing, expect more interview sessions.

**Critical questions to ask at kick-off:**
- "What frameworks or processes do you use with clients that aren't written down anywhere?"
- "What does your best delivery person do that your junior people can't replicate?"
- "What would you want this framework to do for your customers on day one of deployment?"
- "How do you currently onboard a new customer — walk me through every step?"

**Common scoping pitfalls:**
- Assuming IP extraction can be done in one or two sessions — it typically needs 3–5 interviews of 60–90 minutes
- Not accounting for methodology naming time — clients often need a few days to decide
- Treating QA as a formality — if the framework builder has missed anything, QA will catch it and send work back, adding days to the timeline
- Forgetting that the deploy script needs to be tested against a real customer project — this is part of Week 12, not optional

## Signals This is the Right Framework

- Client has real, repeatable IP they currently deliver to clients or students
- They want to scale delivery without proportionally scaling their team
- They understand (or can be educated on) what AI agents actually are
- Their delivery follows a defined journey or methodology — even if informally
- They have a customer base to deploy the framework to once it's built
- They want to own the output permanently, not pay for ongoing AI access
- Revenue is $1M+ and the engagement is commercially justified

---

## Signals This is NOT the Right Framework

- The client wants a single chatbot or automation, not a full framework — scope a standalone AI build instead
- They have no structured IP or delivery process — they need strategy consulting first
- Their primary problem is a revenue constraint in their sales or marketing — route to **Njin Method (TORQUE)** instead
- They don't have 4–6 hours per week to commit — the program requires active client participation especially in Months 1–2
- They want something built for them with no involvement — the done-with-you model requires genuine participation in IP extraction
