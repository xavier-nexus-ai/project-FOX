# Delivery Sequence 2: Njin Method Playbook Delivery

**Name:** Njin Playbook Delivery Sequence
**Applies to:** Njin Method engagements where the primary deliverable is one or more playbooks (Pre-Sales, Sales, Outreach, Ads, Cross-Sell, Referral, or Retention)
**Purpose:** Map the full delivery sequence for a playbook engagement — from initial client onboarding through to 90-day performance review.

---

## Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Primary Activity |
|-------|----------|------------------|
| **Activation - Data Collection** | Week 0 (first 7 days) | Collect Core 12, set up portal, share folder |
| **Activation - Data Validation** | Week 1 | Validate CRM access, historical data, team interviews |
| **Immersion - Week 1-2** | Weeks 2-3 | Tone of Voice generation, LLM project setup |
| **Methodology Creation** | Weeks 2-3 | First playbook draft generated, internal review |
| **Strategic Presentation** | Week 3 | Client review, revisions, final version |
| **CRM Implementation** | Weeks 4-6 | Developer builds automations, workflows, dashboards |
| **Team Training** | Weeks 7-8 | Live training sessions, certification |
| **Live Monitoring** | Weeks 9-12 | Active monitoring, weekly adjustments, Core 12 updates |
| **Handover to Customer Success** | Week 12 | CS takes over ongoing relationship |
| **30-Day CS Review** | Day +30 | First results check |
| **90-Day Performance Review** | Day +90 | Full before/after analysis |

---

## Full Step Sequence

### Phase 1: Client Onboarding & Data Collection (Week 0)

1. **Create Client Portal Entry** — Notion client record, Google Drive folder, LLM project
2. **Send Client Data Collection Form** — Collect Core 12, company basics, tech foundation, owner map, 4 critical qualitative questions
3. **Log The Core 12 in Client Portal** — Creates "before" snapshot, determines primary constraint
4. **Create Client Documentation Folder Structure** — Shared folder with client for uploads
5. **Prerequisite Validation** — CRM access, historical data, team interviews scheduled. If delayed beyond 5 business days, escalate.

### Phase 2: Tone of Voice Creation (Week 1)

6. **Generate Tone of Voice Document** — Use Tone of Voice Analyst GPT, upload client materials, generate profile, client approval required

### Phase 3: LLM Project Setup (Week 1-2)

7. **Create LLM Project** — Claude or ChatGPT Projects, named `[Client] - [Playbook Type] - [Month Year]`
8. **Write System Instructions** — Populated from Core 12, constraint diagnosis, client context, tone of voice
9. **Upload Knowledge Base Documents** — All client materials, Client Portal export, research docs

### Phase 4: Playbook Document Creation (Week 2-3)

10. **Create Master Playbook Document** — Google Doc, named `[Client] - [Playbook Type] - DRAFT v1`
11. **Document Structure - Playbook Framework** — Apply the 20-section framework (Business Overview through AI & Custom Automations)
12. **Adapt Section Depth Based on Playbook Type** — Prioritise sections based on playbook focus

### Phase 5: Content Generation (Week 3)

13. **Use LLM Project to Generate Content** — Section by section, with quality check per section
14. **Internal Review** — PM + SME review, track changes, update to DRAFT v2 then v3
15. **Client Review Round** — 5 business days for feedback, 90-min review call, DRAFT v4

### Phase 6: Finalisation & Handover (Weeks 4-8)

16. **Create Final Deliverable Package** — Master playbook, developer guide, rep handbooks, quick reference, template library, implementation checklist
17. **Developer Handoff** — Weeks 4-6: CRM implementation by dev team
18. **Training Material Handoff** — Weeks 7-8: Team training delivered
19. **Go-Live Preparation** — Pre-launch checklist, automations tested end-to-end

### Phase 7: Active Monitoring (Weeks 9-12)

20. **Active Monitoring** — Weekly CRM activity review, sample call listening, metric tracking, Core 12 actual results captured

### Phase 8: Ongoing Success (Week 12+)

21. **Handover to Customer Success** — CS takes over relationship, ongoing optimisation
22. **30-Day CS Review** — Metrics trending, team adoption, adjustments
23. **90-Day Performance Review** — Full Core 12 update, ROI calculation, next playbook suggestion

---

## Blocking Gates Throughout

- **Data Access Gate** (end of Phase 1) — CRM access, Core 12, team interviews all confirmed
- **Tone of Voice Gate** (end of Phase 2) — Client-approved tone of voice document before any copy is generated
- **Internal Review Gate** (end of Phase 5) — Internal review must pass before client sees the draft
- **Client Approval Gate** (end of Phase 6) — Client sign-off before CRM implementation begins
- **Go-Live Gate** (end of Phase 6) — Pre-launch checklist 100% complete

## Responsible Parties

| Phase | Owner |
|-------|-------|
| Phase 1-2 | PM + Sales (data collection) |
| Phase 3 | PM (LLM setup) |
| Phase 4-5 | PM + SME (content generation, review) |
| Phase 6 | PM (finalisation), Dev team (CRM build), Trainer (team training) |
| Phase 7 | PM (monitoring) |
| Phase 8 | Customer Success AM |

---

## Critical Success Factors

1. **The Core 12 gate everything** — do not proceed past Phase 1 without them
2. **Tone of Voice before copy** — every script, email, and template must sound like the client
3. **Copy-paste ready outputs** — every template must work immediately, no further editing required
4. **Before/after metrics** — the 90-day review is meaningless without a baseline
5. **Client-specific, not generic** — every section references the Core 12 and constraint diagnosis
