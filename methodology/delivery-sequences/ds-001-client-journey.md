# Delivery Sequence 1: Client Journey (Universal)

**Name:** Njin Client Journey — Universal Delivery Sequence
**Applies to:** All client engagements regardless of framework (Njin Method, AI Orchestration, Vibe, Web)
**Purpose:** Map what the client experiences from first contact to ongoing success, with timeline, touchpoints, and responsible parties.

---

## Full Sequence (Client's View)

| # | Stage | What the Client Experiences | When | Who Owns It |
|---|-------|------------------------------|------|-------------|
| 1 | Initial sales call | Books via CRM, meets Njin, hears Activation Promise | Day 0 | Sales |
| 2 | Discovery conversation | Explores their problem in detail | Day 0-1 | Sales |
| 3 | Reverse brief received | Gets written confirmation of problem + cost of inaction | Within 24 business hours | Sales |
| 4 | Scope of Work received | Reviews deliverables (framed as outcomes, includes Earliest Possible Win) | Day 2-3 | Sales/Product |
| 5 | Proposal received | Sees pricing, timeline, final deliverables | Day 3-5 | Sales |
| 6 | Verbal approval | Confirms intent to proceed | Day 5-7 | Sales |
| 7 | Contract received | E-signs contract | Within 1 hour of verbal go-ahead | Operations |
| 8 | Invoice received | Via Xero integration | Immediately after contract | Operations |
| 9 | Payment made | Custom reminder sequence if needed | Within payment terms | Operations |
| 10 | Free gift arrives | "Whilst you enjoy this, we're already building your first automation" | Post-payment (if applicable) | Operations |
| 11 | Dashboard credentials | Receives njin-vibe dashboard access | Post-payment | PMO |

---

## Critical Client-Facing Moments

These are the moments the client remembers. Get these wrong and the engagement breaks.

| Moment | What it demonstrates | Failure mode |
|--------|----------------------|--------------|
| **Reverse brief** | Njin listened and understood | Generic, copy-pasted from last client |
| **Onboarding Activation Call** | Njin is organised and delivery is already in motion | Disorganised, unclear next steps, access not requested |
| **Kickoff Call quick win** | Njin delivers what it promises | No quick win ready — client loses trust on day 1 |
| **Sprint reviews** | Njin generates ROI, not just activity | Status update with no outcomes, no ROI framing |
| **Success report** | The investment paid off | No before/after metrics, no proof |
| **Next-stage proposal** | There's a clear path forward | Generic upsell, no continuity logic |

---

## Timing Anchors

- **24 business hours:** Reverse brief must go out after discovery conversation
- **72 hours:** Onboarding Activation Call after payment
- **1 week:** Kickoff Call after Activation Call
- **Fortnightly:** Sprint cadence throughout delivery
- **End of phase:** Success report + next-stage proposal bundled together

---

## How This Sequence Relates to Framework Delivery

The client journey is the same regardless of which framework governs the internal delivery. What changes is:

- **Njin Method (TORQUE):** Sprints deliver playbook sections, CRM automation, rep training
- **AI Orchestration:** Sprints deliver IP extraction interviews, methodology naming, framework build
- **VIBE OS:** Sprints deliver code increments (stepwise development)
- **AetherFlow:** Sprints deliver website/funnel phases (structure > style > interactions > logic)

In every case, the client sees the same sequence of touchpoints. The internal work is what differs.
