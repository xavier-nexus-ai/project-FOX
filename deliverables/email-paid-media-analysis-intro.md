## Key Findings at a Glance

- **71 to 80% of your website traffic is mobile.** But you're not running any Meta (Facebook/Instagram) ads. That's a big chunk of your audience you're not reaching where they spend their time.
- **Bounce rate is above industry benchmark.** Visit duration is sitting at 4 to 7 seconds across both sites. People are landing and leaving before they can act.
- **Google Ads may be sending traffic to the homepage** instead of dedicated landing pages. This kills conversion because people see too many options and don't know what to do next.
- **Forms are hard to find.** It takes multiple clicks to get to a point where someone can actually enquire. No popups, no chatbot, no instant way to engage.
- **Google Display ads are running.** Display is known for poor performance due to banner blindness. That budget would work harder on Search or Meta.
- **Calculators are buried deep** in the site with too many fields (12 on the mortgage calculator). These should be simplified, gated (capture name, email, phone), and placed front and centre on landing pages as lead magnets.
- **No Microsoft Clarity installed.** This is a free tool that lets you see exactly how users behave on the site. Without it, we're guessing why people leave.
- **No video content.** Your social presence is mostly static posts. Educational video performs much better for engagement and builds trust before someone picks up the phone.
- **Quick fixes needed.** LinkedIn URLs are broken on both sites. There's also a geo-block stopping access from some countries with no clear reason.

Maucir's recommendation is to build a **content funnel on Meta**. This means educational video sequences where someone watches one video, gets retargeted with a deeper one, and by the time they've seen two or three pieces they're warm enough to submit a form. This is how smaller players compete against the Big Four on paid channels without needing their budget.

## The Bigger Point: Digital Leads Are a Different Animal

This is the bit I want you both thinking about.

Right now, most of your pipeline comes through referral partners. Accountants, real estate agents, existing clients sending people your way. Those leads arrive **pre-sold**. The referrer has already done the trust building. They've established your credibility. They've often pre-qualified the need. Your job is to confirm and close. That's why your conversion rates are strong.

Digital leads are the opposite. They've clicked an ad or filled out a form. They've never heard of you. They're probably filling out forms across three other brokers at the same time. They're in research mode, not buying mode.

The data on this is clear:

- **Referral leads convert at 18 to 22%** in financial services
- **Digital leads convert at 2 to 3%** without a structured pre-sale process
- **But with a proper qualification funnel, digital leads can reach 15 to 20%+ conversion.** Finni, an Australian brokerage, hit 80% conversion from booked appointments by pre-qualifying before the broker ever spoke to anyone.

The critical difference is what happens **between the ad click and the broker conversation.** If a digital lead hits your site, fills out a form (assuming they can find one), and then waits for a call back, you've already lost most of them.

The research shows:

- **Responding within 5 minutes makes you 9x more likely to convert** that lead
- **44% of salespeople give up after one follow-up attempt.** Yet **80% of conversions happen after the fifth contact.**
- Digital leads need **automated pre-qualification** (intent, timeline, deposit, employment type) before they should ever reach a broker's calendar

What this means in practice: **you can't run the same playbook for digital leads as you do for referrals.**

A referred lead needs speed and a personalised acknowledgment that references the referrer. A digital lead needs instant AI-driven engagement, automated qualification, and a structured nurture sequence that builds trust over days or weeks before a broker picks up the phone.

The good news is this is exactly what we're building with the pre-sales playbook. The website improvements and paid media strategy Maucir is recommending will generate the leads. But the system that sits behind it, qualifying them, nurturing them, and only putting genuinely ready prospects in front of your brokers, is what turns a 2% conversion rate into a 15 to 20% one.
