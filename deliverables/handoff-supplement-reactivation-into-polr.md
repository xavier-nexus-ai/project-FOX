## THE REACTIVATION OPPORTUNITY

### The Numbers
- 49,000 leads/year generated by FFG and sold to the lead market for pennies
- These people already raised their hand. They already know Fox.
- Repeat customers convert at 40-45% vs 5-10% cold
- Even at 2% conversion: ~980 deals/year = ~$2.25M in FFG commission alone
- Currently: ZERO reactivation. Every declined or ghosted lead is thrown away.
- 90-day plan for declined customers already converts at 20% (this exists but isn't systematised)
- Customer care calls generated 41 new enquiries last month with no automation at all

### Why Reactivation Goes Into POLR (Not a Separate Pipeline)
Reactivation leads are not new leads. They don't need the T-System pre-sales cadence. They need VALUE first, then a natural moment where Fox becomes relevant again. That's exactly what POLR does.

A declined FFG lead from 6 months ago who gets brought into POLR:
- Gets financial wellness content (builds trust that was damaged by the decline)
- Gets tools and calculators (re-engages them with Fox without selling)
- Hits a trigger point where their circumstances may have changed
- Gets handed off to FFG pre-sales ONLY when they signal readiness

The POLR journey handles the "warming up" that a traditional reactivation campaign would do manually. The difference is POLR does it as part of a continuous value journey, not a one-off blast.

### Reactivation Segments
Not all 49K are the same. Segment by:

**By Recency:**
| Segment | Size (est.) | Priority | Approach |
|---------|-------------|----------|----------|
| 0-6 months | ~24,500 | Highest | Recent memory of Fox. Highest conversion potential. |
| 6-12 months | ~12,250 | High | May have settled elsewhere. Life circumstances may have changed. |
| 12-24 months | ~8,200 | Medium | Longer gap. Need stronger value hook to re-engage. |
| 24+ months | ~4,050 | Lower | Cold. Only worth including in broad POLR value content. |

**By Outcome:**
| Segment | Approach |
|---------|----------|
| Declined (credit/income) | "Circumstances change. Let's check if things look different now." Lead with 90-day credit improvement plan. |
| Declined (product mismatch) | "We've added new lenders and products since we last spoke." |
| Ghosted (no response) | "No pressure. We're here when you're ready." Value content only. |
| Withdrew (changed mind) | "Still thinking about it? Here's what's changed in the market." |
| Settled elsewhere | "Congratulations on your loan. We can still help with your next one." Long-term value play. |

**By Business:**
| Source | POLR Entry Point | Potential Handoff |
|--------|------------------|-------------------|
| FFG declined | POLR → FFG value track | FFG pre-sales when ready |
| FFG ghosted | POLR → general value track | FFG or FHL depending on signals |
| FHL declined | POLR → FHL value track | FHL pre-sales when ready |
| FHL ghosted | POLR → general value track | FHL or FFG depending on signals |

## COMMUNICATION PRINCIPLES FOR REACTIVATION

### The Golden Rules
1. **Value first, always.** Never open with "we noticed you didn't finish your application." Open with "we built some free tools you might find useful."
2. **Never reference the decline directly.** Not "you were declined" but "circumstances change" or "things may look different now."
3. **Let them self-discover.** Give them calculators and tools so THEY realise they might now qualify. Don't tell them — let the tool show them.
4. **Monthly cadence maximum.** These people already stopped engaging once. Don't overwhelm them.
5. **Easy unsubscribe.** Make it effortless to opt out. Respect signals.
6. **90-day cooling period for declines.** Compliance and decency. Don't re-engage someone who was declined less than 3 months ago.
7. **Warm handoff, not auto-enroll.** When they signal readiness, create a task for a human broker. Don't auto-submit them into the pre-sales T-System as a cold lead.
8. **Carry context forward.** When handing off to FFG/FHL pre-sales, include their history: original outcome, time elapsed, what POLR content they engaged with, and what changed. The broker should never ask "so what are you looking for?" — they should already know.

### Tone by Segment
| Segment | Tone | Example Opening |
|---------|------|-----------------|
| Recently declined (0-6mo) | Careful, empowering | "We've put together some tools to help you understand your financial position. No commitment, no pressure." |
| Ghosted (any recency) | Casual, no guilt | "We're still here if you ever need us. In the meantime, here's something you might find useful." |
| Older leads (12mo+) | Fresh start | "A lot has changed in finance over the past year. Here are some free tools to see where you stand." |
| Settled elsewhere | Generous, long-game | "Congratulations on your loan. We can still help with future needs — here's a free property tracker." |
