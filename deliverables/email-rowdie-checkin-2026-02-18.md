## What POLR is looking to do (quick version)

In short, POLR is a custom-built platform that sits alongside your existing systems (Ambition, Finshaw's/Connective, UMI). It doesn't replace them - it connects them. The core pieces:

- **One unified database** pulling contacts from all three businesses so you can actually see the full customer picture
- **Automated lifecycle journeys** - post-settlement nurture, cross-sell triggers, lead market reactivation, care call prioritisation
- **A team dashboard** so your team can see cross-sell opportunities, automation status, and customer lifecycle stage without logging into another system
- **Email and SMS management** - template editing, bulk sends with proper segmentation, and nurture sequence management all in one place
- **Tagging and overlap detection** - automatically flagging when a customer has relationships across FFG, FHL, or UMI

The full detailed scope will be with you by end of week once the developers confirm timings and we align everything under the existing pricing.
