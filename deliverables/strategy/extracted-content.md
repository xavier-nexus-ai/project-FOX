## 6. Settlement Calls Key Points

**Source:** Settlement Calls Key Points.docx (updated 2026-03-27, image-based document)

### Settlement Call Checklist (Dealer)

1. Excitedly congratulate the dealer on you both being able to help this customer together.
2. Thank them for being so helpful throughout the process with getting information to you.
3. Let them know that payment will usually happen overnight (on a business day).
4. This customer was a little outside the box for most lenders, but we were still able to help them get affordable finance.
5. I'd love to help more of your customers in the future. If it's ok, I'll email you my best contact details, so you have them handy for next time.
6. Let them know that if you can get regular referrals from them that you would be happy to sit down with your manager and see what referral program and commission you could put in place for every customer that settles a loan with us that they send our way.

**Key insight:** Settlement call to the dealer is positioned as a relationship-building and referral-generation moment, not just a transactional update. Ties directly into the affiliate/referral ecosystem strategy.
