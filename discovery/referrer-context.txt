Current setup

Using SendGrid as a pseudo CRM with lists and “hoppers” for different lead outcomes

Actual deal flow happens in the aggregator loan management systems (Ambition etc) for each team

Around 50,000 contacts in total across Fox Finance Group and UME

Segmentation and nurture are pretty clunky, and they cannot easily get specific with who gets what

What they want from a CRM layer

Proper marketing automation and segmentation

Build real evergreen nurture journeys based on:

Type of enquiry (asset, commercial, home loans, bad credit, SME with ABN, etc)

Stage of the journey (new lead, no docs returned, unable to contact, abandoned application, settled customer)

Much better list management and segmentation, so they are not just blasting broad EDMs to the whole list

Ability to use engagement data to trigger different branches in the journey

Lead scoring and sales handover

Track opens, clicks, reply behaviour, webinar registrations, etc and turn that into a usable lead score

Surface hot leads for the sales team with simple call queues, rather than having them trawl through lists

Daily lists of “people to call” for sales, based on engagement score and behaviour (like BAP) 

Pre sales pipeline before the loan systems

An SDR pipeline like the one you built for us in GHL, that sits before their loan management systems

Handle the marketing leads, then pass qualified opportunities into Ambition and their other loan CRMs

Abandoned application follow up

They track “started vs completed” on the Apply Now form in GA4

Plan is to capture name, email and phone number on the very first step, then:

Trigger immediate SMS and email when someone starts but does not complete

Drop those contacts into a short, focused sequence to help them finish the application and answer common objections

Ideally, this creates a queue for a salesperson or client service person to call these people quickly

Client service and post settlement workflows

They are thinking about a client service role similar to what Bec does for us, whose job would be to:

Re engage people who stalled in the process

Call customers a month after settlement to check everything went smoothly, reinforce the relationship, and remind them about the Refer a Friend program

Schedule future touch points at 6, 12, 18, 24 months, supported by automated emails and SMS ahead of the call

They want all of this to be driven by the CRM, with tasks and call lists generated automatically

Single view across two businesses

Two brands: Fox Finance Group (assets and commercial) and Fox Home Loans (mortgages)

They want a way for the CRM to sit between those brands so they can see:

When a customer has had both an asset loan and a home loan

History of interactions across both, in one place

They already know their loan systems can integrate via API or Zapier and just need to understand how GHL could be structured here

Scaling for organic and paid leads

Right now, most enquiry is organic inbound and referral based, with very strong conversion from repeat and referral customers

They are planning to ramp up paid traffic, so they want the CRM and automations in place before they pour more volume into the top of the funnel
