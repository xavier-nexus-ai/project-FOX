## Client Goals & Expectations

- **Revenue target:** $2.35M (FFG) by end of next 12 months - a $400K gap (~21% growth)
- **June milestone:** $200K monthly income
- **Vision:** Both businesses operating as one group - every customer gets access to both services
- **CRM expectation:** Single unified customer view across FFG and FHL, with proper segmentation, lead scoring, and automated lifecycle nurture
- **SDR expectation:** A dedicated person operating out of the CRM to convert abandoned applications and re-engage cold leads
- **Cross-sell expectation:** Automated triggers at settlement milestones, team-assisted handoffs between businesses, and a natural "human-led" introduction approach (not hard-sell)
- **Branding refresh:** New branding and website builds planned for 2026 - good timing for alignment

## What We Already Know Well

- The two LMS systems (Ambition for FFG, Infinity for FHL) don't talk to each other
- Cross-sell currently depends on individual initiative - no system, no tracking
- SendGrid is being used as a pseudo-CRM with basic lists and "hoppers"
- EDMs sent to ~1K customers get only 2-3 responses due to poor targeting
- Engagement drops off at 6 months across their 12 common automations (open rate falls from 50-75% to 15-20%)
- Bad credit form sees 400+ entries/week with ~50% completion but no follow-up for drop-offs
- $50K/year in onsold leads that are never re-engaged
- 90-day plan customers (bad credit on a fix path) respond well when prompted - trigger-based nurture works
- Cross-sell compliance is fine - signed privacy and consent covers both businesses
- The handoff approach: FFG introduces the customer to FHL (not FHL reaching out cold)
- Compliance restrictions: no "advice", "guarantee", "financial hardship"
- Commercial refinance within 12 months has early payout fees; home loan refinance before 18 months has clawback
- Team receives gift cards when cross-sell loans settle
- 42 data fields available across both LMS systems for customer profiling
- Nathan prefers focused execution - one playbook at a time, don't bite off too much

### 3. The Cross-Sell Journey (Deep Dive)

This is the core of Playbook 1. Need to map out:

- Walk me through what happens when an FFG customer settles today - step by step, who does what?

- How does the handoff actually work when it goes well? Can you give me a real example?
- What does the team say that works? What falls flat?
- When a customer comes back for a second product, what triggered it? (Did they call? Did someone reach out? Did an EDM land?)
- You mentioned different cross-sell paths depending on customer type - can we map out the top 3-4 most common scenarios?

### 4. Customer Segmentation Reality

- Of the 50K contacts, roughly how many are: active FFG customers / active FHL customers / both / old leads / onsold/disqualified?

### 5. Team Workflow & Capacity

- Who will be the primary user of the CRM day-to-day?
- Is the dedicated client services/SDR person hired yet, or is that dependent on this project?

## Questions for Playbook 1: Cross-Sell & Monetisation

1. "Walk me through the last successful cross-sell - from first contact to settlement. What happened?"
2. "What's the most natural moment to introduce the other business? At application? At settlement? At the 1-month check-in?"
3. "You said the team has scripting for cross-sell - can I see it? What works and what doesn't?"
4. "If I'm an FFG customer who just settled a car loan, what would the ideal next 12 months of communication look like from your perspective?"
5. "You mentioned customers with a mortgage have more cross-sell options (refinance + new property). How do you identify who has a mortgage today?"
6. "The 90-day plan re-engagement works well - what specifically triggers the customer to come back? Can we replicate that pattern?"
7. "What does a 'warm intro' from FFG to FHL actually sound like on the phone? Can you role-play it for me?"
8. "You said 10 cross-sells per team per month is the target - where are you actually tracking against that? Is it written down or just a verbal goal?"
9. "What stops the team from doing more cross-sells? Is it awareness, time, tools, or confidence?"

## Questions for Playbook 2: Pre-Sales SDR

1. "The bad credit form gets 400+ entries per week - what happens to the ones who complete but don't proceed? And the ones who drop off?"
2. "You mentioned $50K/year in onsold leads that are never touched again - what does that pipeline look like? Where do they go?"
3. "If we built an SDR pipeline, who would sit in that seat? Is that a new hire or is someone being redeployed?"
4. "What's the current response time when a new lead comes in? Who picks it up?"
5. "How do you decide if a lead is worth a phone call vs just getting an automated sequence?"
6. "For abandoned applications - you capture name, email, phone on step one. What happens with that data today?"

## Questions About Both Businesses

1. "Are the ICPs genuinely different between FFG and FHL, or is there more overlap than you think?"
2. "Which business has more room to grow - FFG with better systems, or FHL which is already growing at 107%?"
3. "If we could only fix one thing in the next 90 days, what would make the biggest difference to revenue?"
4. "The branding refresh - will the two businesses stay as separate brands, or is there a chance they merge under one brand?"
5. "Who on the team is most excited about this? Who's most sceptical? That helps us know where to focus training."

1. **Catch-up & alignment** (10 min) - Where are we, confirm priorities, set agenda

6. **Voice & messaging** (15 min) - Collect samples, discuss tone, schedule formal extraction if needed
7. **Next steps & access** (10 min) - Confirm platform access, schedule follow-ups, set immersion week activities
