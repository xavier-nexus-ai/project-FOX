### Home Loans Value-Add Opportunities

1. **Repricing** (can be done every 3 months):
   - Go back to current bank to negotiate better rate
   - Example: LVR drops from 83% to 75% as property value increases
   - Difference between 80% and 75% LVR rate could be half a percent
   - Bank won't proactively offer better rate
   - Broker negotiates: "save me moving customer to another bank"
   - No commission but builds massive value
   - Currently done on longer intervals, talking about every 3 months
   - Digital process: log into lender system, request repricing with story
   - Can get answer same day

2. **Free Property Valuation** (between 6-12 months):
   - Subscription tool they already pay for
   - Can send within a minute
   - Shows equity, racing styles, area data
   - Customer might want to do renos, invest, or just check equity
   - Pushes into different LVR bracket = better rate opportunity

3. **Refinance** (outside 18-month clawback):
   - Move to different bank for better deal
   - If done within 12-18 months: original bank claws back 100% of commission
   - Effectively doing second deal for free
   - After clawback period: full new commission opportunity
