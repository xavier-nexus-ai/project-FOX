### Key Decisions

**Simplified V1 Approach Agreed** 

- Focus exclusively on FHL 18-month nurture sequence first
- Shelve pre-sale playbook for now (only needed if running ads immediately)
- Add FFG cross-selling features in V2 after V1 proves value
- Polar recognized as two separate playbooks: customer success and cross-selling

**Custom System vs GHL Debate** 

- James strongly recommends custom system over GoHighLevel due to complexity requirements
- GHL cannot handle the level of dynamic customer journey personalization needed
- GHL dashboarding is poor and system becomes messy with complex logic
- Nate remains cautious due to past negative experiences with technology partners and wants quick wins

**Risk Mitigation Strategy** 

- James offers: prototype the middle path, and if Nate doesn't like it, will build in GHL at no extra cost
- This gives Nate visibility before commitment while demonstrating feasibility

### System Design Discussion

**Polar System Scope Clarified** 

- Polar is the entire ecosystem, not just one database or playbook
- Includes aggregator platforms, scripting, nurture sequences, and cross-business systems
- Core value proposition: positioning Fox as "the guide" to keep customers sticky long-term
- This is critical for staying relevant as loan services become more automated

**Customer Journey Complexity** 

- 18-month FHL nurture sequence is the "moneymaker" with repricing, refinancing touchpoints
- FFG journey is lighter/lifestyle-focused with less hand-holding needed
- Goal: everyone enters through FFG or FHL, gets nurtured, then crosses to other business at 9-month mark when credit score improves
- System needs to be dynamic and account for multiple ICAs, not cookie-cutter single journey

**ICA (Ideal Customer Avatar) Framework** 

- Five ICAs defined for FFG, mapped to corresponding FHL purchase types
- Prime convenience borrower (unsecured loans $30k+) identified as highest-value ICA
- Provides best ROI due to speed, smooth experience, and ability to create raving fans
- All customer data captured during application process via Ambition (current aggregator)

### Technical Considerations

**Data Capture & Integration** 

- Current aggregator (Ambition) provides extensive customer data for asset finance
- Home loans aggregator lacks similar reporting capabilities
- Considering switch to new aggregator (LMG) - need to confirm API capabilities Thursday
- Team can capture ICA-relevant data upfront during application to improve nurture targeting

**AI & Automation Tools Discussed** 

- James demonstrated Claude Code workflow using folder-based methodology with pre-prompted agents
- Rowdie building operational AI agent system for business functions (marketing, leadership, HR, L&D)
- Both using vibe coding for rapid prototyping and stakeholder buy-in before full development

### Strategic Context

**Pre-Sale Playbook Research** 

- Most finance businesses in this sector rely purely on referrals/word of mouth
- Online conversion rates typically 2-5% - industry just accepts this
- Fox is set up for hot leads but struggles with ads because cold leads need different nurturing
- Calculator-based lead magnets identified as strong entry point if ads are pursued

**Reactivation Identified as Biggest Opportunity** 

- Reactivating existing leads and clients is the largest untapped opportunity
- Two types: reactivating cold leads and reactivating past clients
- Client-side reactivation overlaps with customer success playbook

**Competitive Positioning** 

- Digital product (Polar) is the "real thing that will keep people sticky"
- Question driving strategy: "How do we stay relevant when loans can be fully automated?"
- Answer: Provide ongoing value as a guide, not just transactional loan service
- FFG is the best lead magnet for FHL since home loans are harder to advertise directly
