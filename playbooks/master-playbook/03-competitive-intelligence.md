## Our Differentiation

**Unique Position:** The new 12-month, ICA-specific post-settlement system (built in GHL, content driven by Fox-MIS) is what makes the FFG and FHL combination earn money instead of just sitting there.

**Win Themes:**

1. **Proactive value, not reactive service.** Fox sends property valuations unprompted via Cotality. Fox advocates for better rates without being asked. Competitors wait for the client to call.

2. **ICA-specific, not one-size-fits-all.** Five separate journeys. Each with its own content, tone, and timing. A young first-time car loan customer rebuilding credit gets a different journey from a prime repeat borrower in their 50s. Most competitors send the same generic email to everyone. The data on what that produces is in the FFG sequence: 187 unique clicks across 5,456 sends.

3. **Value for non-clients.** The system delivers Cotality valuations, rate intelligence, and education content. Most competitors will not give away this level of value. Build trust first. Earn the business later.

## Battle Cards

### vs Dealership Finance
- **They say:** "We can sort your finance right here, right now."
- **We say:** "We compare 50+ lenders to find the right fit, not just the one sitting in front of you."
- **Exploit:** Dealership finance is convenience-driven with higher rates and no ongoing relationship. They cannot cross-sell home loans or help with debt consolidation.

### vs Big-Brand Mortgage Brokers (Aussie, Mortgage Choice)
- **They say:** "Australia's leading mortgage broker with access to hundreds of lenders."
- **We say:** "Same lender access, plus we check in with property valuations and rate reviews without you asking. And if you need a car loan or equipment finance, we handle that too."
- **Exploit:** Franchise model means inconsistent service quality. No asset finance arm. No proactive lifecycle management. Settlement is the end of the relationship.

### vs Bank Direct
- **They say:** "Bank with us for everything, one place for all your money."
- **We say:** "We compare all the banks to find which one works for you, then we keep checking to make sure they're still the right fit."
- **Exploit:** Banks are limited to their own products. They won't tell you a competitor has a better rate. They don't proactively reprice. And they definitely don't offer sub-prime lending for when things get tough.

### vs Online Comparison Sites
- **They say:** "Compare rates from dozens of lenders in minutes."
- **We say:** "We compare too, but then we walk you through the whole process, settle the deal, and keep looking after you for years."
- **Exploit:** Comparison sites generate leads and sell them. There is no human, no relationship, and no follow-up. Once you click "apply", you're on your own.
